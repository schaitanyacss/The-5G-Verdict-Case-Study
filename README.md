## 📖 Background & Overview

**Wavecon**, a telecom provider with roughly a **20% market share**, underwent a nine-month transformation from 3G/4G to 5G between **January and September 2022**.

This project analyzes the impact of the 5G rollout on **revenue, market share, and plan performance** to determine why heavy 5G investment did not immediately translate into total revenue growth.

---

## 🗂 Data Structure Overview

The analysis covers three distinct phases:

| Phase | Period |
|---|---|
| 3G/4G Era | Jan – Apr |
| Transition | May |
| 5G Era | Jun – Sep |

**Key metrics tracked across these periods:**
- Revenue
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
- Active User base (TAU)
- Churn Rate (TUsU)
- Market Share

---

## 💡 Executive Summary

The 5G launch represented a **"Value vs. Volume" trade-off**:

- ✅ Successfully drove higher spending per customer: **+11.05% ARPU**
- ❌ Failed to increase total revenue: **-0.5%**

This stagnation is driven by a **shrinking user base (-8.28% active users)** and a **significant spike in churn (+23.5%)**, suggesting that while loyal users are spending more, the company is losing its broader customer base.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### Market Share Erosion
- Wavecon experienced a **3.13-point drop in market share** (from 20.97% to 17.84%) immediately following the 5G launch, coinciding with aggressive competitor acquisition pushes.

### Plan Performance
- Flagship 5G plans (**P11, P12**) saw rapid adoption, contributing **10% of total revenue in just four months**.
- Legacy plans like **P7** saw a **73% decline** as they lose relevance to unlimited 5G offers.

### City-Level Risk
- The **top 5 cities** generate **~60% of revenue** but are facing active-user declines.
- Cities like **Pune** and **Chennai** show **"discount-risk"** — ARPU is falling despite user growth.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations

| Recommendation | Action |
|---|---|
| **Retire/Redesign Plans** | Discontinue legacy plans **P5** and **P7**, which 5G has made obsolete and redesign **P4** and **P6** to gain traction. |
| **Scale Winners** | Redirect marketing spend of retired plans towards flagship 5G plans (**P11, P12**) and established revenue engines (**P1, P2, P3**). |
| **Targeted Retention** | Launch targeted 5G retention offers in highest-churn top-5 cities |
| **Reinvest** | Prioritize network investment in bottom-5 ARPU cities |
| **Market Positioning** | Launch SIM-porting campaigns and device-upgrade offers in cities having significant competitive friction. |
